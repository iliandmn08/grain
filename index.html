01 — The problem

Gardeners water by habit, not by data

💧

Wasted water

Timers run on a fixed schedule: same time, same amount, rain or heatwave.
🌵

Drought rules

In past droughts Catalonia limited garden watering to 2 days a week; Seville banned it with drinking water.
🥀

No expert advice

Which fertiliser? Why are the leaves yellow? People guess, google or ask a neighbour.

13 — Key risks

What could go wrong, and our answer

🔌

Sensors won't connect

We start with brands that offer an open connection, and our own kit works with any garden.
💶

People won't pay

Freemium: users see the litres and euros they save before they upgrade. The pilot measures it.
🏢

Big brands copy us

They only work with their own hardware; we work with all of them and move faster.
🔒

Users' data

GDPR-compliant from day one: data stored in the EU, never sold.
